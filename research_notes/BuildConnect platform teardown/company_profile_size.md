## Who operates buildconnect.com, and which country and region is it focused on?

### Takeaway
buildconnect.com is operated by **MSRBuildConnect LLC**, a Washington State LLC headquartered in Lynnwood, WA (Seattle metro, USA). Its site footer also uses the name "Build Connect Inc." It is a sister business of the homebuilder **MSR Communities**: the two share a building and nearly the same leadership roster, and both sit inside the **MSRcosmos Group**, a US-based conglomerate whose flagship is an SAP/Azure IT-services firm. The market is the US, not the GCC, UK or India.

### Inferences
- The operator is the MSRcosmos/MSR Communities founder group, not an independent startup. The evidence: the same building, an overlapping leadership roster (see the leadership section), testimonials only from MSR Communities, and a sister "MSR…connect" entity formed the same day under the group chairman.
- MSRcosmos sells SAP-on-Azure services ([MSRcosmos About](https://www.msrcosmos.com/about-us/)), which fits the all-Azure hosting.
- The focus is the US, specifically the Puget Sound / Seattle region (Lynnwood/Bothell, WA): Washington governing law, a 206 phone number, USD pricing, US-style homebuilder memberships, and 54.39% US traffic (see the size section).
- The "destination port" installment term suggests imported materials, probably containerized imports of quartz, SPC flooring, cabinets and similar products. The origin country is not stated anywhere.
- Build Connect very likely acts as a captive procurement and import arm for MSR Communities' home building, opened up to outside B2B/B2C buyers.
- "Build Connect Inc." in the footer does not match the registered "MSRBuildConnect LLC". The Instagram handle is "build_connect_llc". The footer line is probably a brand or copyright artifact, unless a separate corporation exists that I did not find.

### Gaps
- No Washington SOS record for a corporation named "Build Connect Inc." was found. The official CCFS search (ccfs.sos.wa.gov) returned a bot-check ("System verification in progress") to scripted requests, so the registry mirror was used instead. Its date, governor and document number should be re-checked on CCFS.
- WHOIS/RDAP registrant data is masked by Domains By Proxy, LLC, so domain ownership cannot be tied to the LLC from RDAP alone.
- The memberships the site claims ("Proud Member of" Build Green, NAHB, Quality Builders Warranty) were not verified with those organizations. They may belong to MSR Communities as a builder rather than to Build Connect.
- Supplier and country of origin for products are not disclosed.
